# Steps to Overcome the Obstacle

To overcome the challenge of my CGPA, I plan to start small and gradually
build up:

1. Focus on improving my grades one semester at a time instead of trying to
   fix everything at once.
2. Identify weak subjects and dedicate extra study time to them.
3. Seek help from teachers, mentors, or tutoring resources when needed.
4. Build consistent study habits rather than relying on last-minute effort.
5. Gain relevant experience (tutoring, volunteering, teaching assistant
   roles) to strengthen my profile beyond just CGPA.
6. Set small, achievable milestones and track progress regularly so
   improvement feels manageable rather than overwhelming.

By starting small and staying consistent, I believe I can steadily work my
way toward my goal of becoming a teacher.
